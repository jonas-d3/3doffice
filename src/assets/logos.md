# Monitor logos

Downloaded on 2026-10-16. The original logos are kept locally and fitted to a monitor canvas without stretching. The FCK preview's empty outer margins are omitted when drawing it.

- `fck-logo.png`: [F.C. København's official design guide](https://design.fck.dk/), [blue primary logo asset](https://res.cloudinary.com/brandpad/image/upload/c_scale,w_768/v1714103781/28337/logo-blue). Displayed on Lars' monitor, east side of island 2, southern seat.
- `satyricon-logo.png`: [Satyricon's official website](https://www.satyriconofficial.com/), [logo asset](https://www.satyriconofficial.com/images/logo.png). Displayed on Henrik's monitor, east side of island 0, northern seat.

Seat locations are taken from `ressources/locations.png`. Logos remain the property of their respective owners.
