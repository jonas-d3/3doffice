# Apply cape logo

`ressources/locations.png` identifies the Apply logo on the kitchen wall. The photos `ressources/kitchen/2026-10-07 09.25.48.jpg` and `ressources/kitchen/2026-10-07 09.26.08.jpg` show the short **a:** monogram. The cape uses this version, not the full wordmark.

`apply-monogram.svg` reuses the a and colon vector contours from the official wordmark below, with the colon placed beside the a to match the photographed sign.

`apply-wordmark.svg` is the official Apply Agency wordmark, downloaded on 2026-10-16 from [apply.agency/brand/apply-wordmark-2.svg](https://apply.agency/brand/apply-wordmark-2.svg), as linked by [Apply Agency](https://apply.agency/).

The visitor's green cape uses the monogram as an off-white print. Its texture is generated locally, follows the cape's folds and is only printed on the outside. The logo remains the property of its owner.
